## 📌 Problem Statement

Traditional brick-and-mortar optical businesses face substantial challenges in modern retail:
1. **Physical Try-On Bottleneck**: Customers struggle to visualize how frames look without visiting physical showrooms.
2. **Complex Lens Customization**: Buying prescription lenses online is often confusing and prone to power calculation errors.
3. **Fragmented Prescription Management**: Patients frequently lose physical paper prescriptions and lack centralized optical history.
4. **Appointment & Queue Inefficiencies**: Lack of automated scheduling leads to clinic waiting times for eye checkups and consultations.
5. **Operational Silos**: Disconnected inventory, sales, lab orders, and customer management tools.

---

## 💡 The Solution

**Nayantara Opticals** solves these challenges by combining state-of-the-art web technology, interactive virtual try-on, clinical appointment scheduling, and automated prescription processing into a single seamless platform.

## 🚀 Core Capabilities & Highlights

### 🛍️ Smart Eyewear Experience
- **Interactive Virtual Try-On**: Try frames and sunglasses virtually before purchasing.
- **Smart Face-Shape Quiz**: Recommends best-fit frame styles based on face geometry, lifestyle, and optical power.
- **Advanced Lens Configurator**: Multi-tier selection for Single Vision, Bi-Focal, Progressive, Blue-Cut, Anti-Glare, and Photochromic lenses.

### 🩺 Eyecare Clinic & Appointments
- **Real-Time Doctor Booking**: Dedicated slots for comprehensive eye tests, myopia management, and frame styling sessions.
- **Digital Prescription Vault**: Secure upload and storage of medical optical records with prescription verification workflows.

### 📊 Retail & Enterprise Management
- **Role-Based Operations**: Dedicated, protected portals for Customers, Clinic Staff, Store Owners, and Super Admins.
- **Real-Time Analytics**: Insights into eyewear trends, top-selling frames, customer retention, and clinic footfall.
- **Inventory & Order Workflow**: End-to-end tracking from frame selection to prescription verification and final delivery.

## 🛠️ Technology Highlights

- **Frontend Interface**: React 19, Modern Responsive Design System, Tailwind CSS, Lucide UI.
- **Backend Architecture**: Node.js, Express, TypeScript, Enterprise RBAC & Security Middleware.
- **Database Layer**: Managed PostgreSQL (Supabase), Prisma ORM with connection pooling.
- **Security & Reliability**: End-to-end data encryption, Rate limiting, Secure token management, and S3-compatible medical vault.
